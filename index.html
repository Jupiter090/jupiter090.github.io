Something interesting about me...

Hi, I'm Matej and I'm from Slovakia. I'm currently studying at a high school. My hobbies: quite obviously I'm interested in computers as a whole but mainly the software side of things but that doesn't mean, that I don't have any knowledge about hardware. On the other side I quite like going on walks as a method of relaxing and clearing my mind. I've gained interest in programming about 5-6 years ago and also started to learn it around that time. First language and the one that I use the most often to this day is C#. Back then I only made simple console applications, but I slowly moved my interest to making games in Unity. Along the way I also gained some knowledge in other parts of software development like Python and web development.

My journey so far

My journey began in my childhood. I was really interested in computers and how they work since I was a kid. And that curiosity just grew and grew over the years, until the year 2020, when I saw a tutorial on YouTube on how to code in C#. It was something that I always wanted to try so I wasted no time and got right into it, but at first I wasn't actually learning anything, just copying tutorials and because of that I gave up on coding. But after a few months I discovered this mobile app, which was able to teach me how to make websites and I downloaded it right away. It took me a bit of time but eventually I was able to finish every lesson in that app and made my first website. It wasn't anything spectacular, but I was proud of myself because I just made something built completely by me without the help of tutorials. After this achievement I made the step to try and learn C# again, but this time actually trying to understand what each thing meant. And it paid off, I was able to make projects by myself. Afterwards I experimented a bit with C# like console applications, a web app with a C# backend, windows forms apps, but eventually I landed on Unity where I spent most of my time. I've been learning Unity ever since and already finished a few smaller games, which you will be able to see at the projects page so go check it out :). Alongside learning C# and Unity I also tried other languages like Python or Java, but I haven't reached a level where I would be able to make what I would consider 'good code'.
